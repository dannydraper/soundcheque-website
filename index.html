Features

Everything a gig invoice needs, nothing it doesn't.

🎤

Built for gig work

The step-by-step wizard asks how many sets you played, how long each one ran, and your total fee — then splits it into line items automatically.

🧾

Six invoice layouts

From a clean Classic design to a bold Sidebar or an Elegant Centered layout for weddings — pick the one that matches how you work.

🎨

Your own colour

Eight accent colours to choose from, applied across every invoice you send — so it looks like your business, not a template.

📇

Pull from Contacts

Choose a client straight from your phone's contacts, or pick from clients you've already billed — no retyping an email address twice.

📤

Send or save, your call

Email the finished PDF straight from the app, or save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere else you keep your paperwork.

✏️

Edit anytime

Spotted a typo after you've generated the PDF? Edit the invoice in place — no deleting and starting over, no wasted invoice number.

Colour options

Pick a colour, keep it consistent.

Every layout adapts to whichever accent colour you choose in Settings — the same eight options, used consistently across your invoice history.
